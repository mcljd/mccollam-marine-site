## Professional Naval Architecture

McCollam Marine provides naval architecture and boat design services to boat builders, yards, commercial operators and private clients. Our work is grounded in practical engineering knowledge and a thorough understanding of the regulatory environment — producing documentation that is accurate, clear and ready for use by builders, surveyors and certifying authorities.

## Design Services

- Concept design and feasibility studies
- Lines plans and hull form development
- General arrangement and layout drawings
- Structural design and scantling calculations
- Specification writing for new builds
- Conversion and modification design support
- Stability and performance analysis
- Class and flag state submission support

## Vessel Types

- **Commercial Vessels** — Workboats, pilot vessels, ferries and passenger craft designed for commercial operations.
- **Fishing Vessels** — New build design and conversion support for commercial fishing vessels.
- **Leisure Craft** — Custom design and technical support for leisure motor boats and sailing yachts.
- **Specialist Craft** — Patrol vessels, rescue craft and other specialist applications requiring bespoke design solutions.
