## Body content

McCollam Marine can carry out all aspects of Stability Tests and produce Stability
Books if necessary for vessels.

All fishing vessels over 15m are required to have a Lightship Check carried out every
5 years to ensure that the vessel is still within acceptable limits on Displacement
and LCG. McCollam Marine is authorised by the MCA to carry out these checks and to
issue the reports to the MCA, enabling the vessel to have a current certificate issued.

If a vessel fails its Lightship Check, an Inclining Test will need to be carried out
to establish if the vessel still meets statutory criteria, and if necessary an
amendment to the stability book can be carried out.
