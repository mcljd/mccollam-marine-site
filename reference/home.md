## Body content

McCollam Marine is a Naval Architecture and Marine Surveying business which caters
for a wide range of vessel types and operations on both the commercial and leisure
fleet throughout Ireland, UK, Europe and Worldwide. The company has 20+ years
experience in Naval Architecture, Boat Building, Boat Surveying and Consultancy in
the marine industry. Based in the beautiful Glens of Antrim on the North Coast of Ireland.

Services include Passenger Boat Coding, MCA Under 15m Fishing Vessel Registration
Inspections, Pre-Purchase Surveys, Insurance Surveys, Damage Surveys and Stability
Calculations. McCollam Marine is an authorised surveyor for the major Certifying
Authorities including the YDSA, YBDSA, Mecal, SEAFISH and the MCA.
