## Services section — "What We Do / Expert Marine Services"

From initial concept through to full certification — McCollam Marine provides the technical expertise and regulatory knowledge your vessel project demands.

1. **Passenger Boat Coding** — Guidance and survey support for passenger vessel coding and compliance with all applicable Codes of Practice.
2. **Boat Surveys** — Pre-purchase, insurance and condition surveys across all vessel types — accurate, thorough and independently delivered.
3. **Naval Architecture** — Design support, structural input and full documentation from concept through to delivery — built for real use.
4. **Stability** — Stability calculations and documentation aligned with your vessel's operational profile and regulatory requirements.
5. **Fishing Vessels** — Specialist services for commercial fishing vessels — surveys, compliance support and inspection requirements.
6. **Tonnage Certification** — Tonnage measurement and certification for vessel registration and compliance across all regulatory frameworks.
