## About McCollam Marine

McCollam Marine was established in 2011 to provide Naval Architecture and Marine Surveying services to the Commercial and Leisure Boat Fleet throughout Northern Ireland, Ireland, the UK, Europe and worldwide. Based in the stunning Glens of Antrim on Northern Ireland's north coast, the company brings together decades of hands-on maritime experience with a deep commitment to technical precision and regulatory compliance.

Every project — from a pre-purchase survey on a leisure yacht to a full passenger vessel coding programme — is delivered with the same attention to detail, practical knowledge and clear communication that has made McCollam Marine a trusted name in the industry.

## Meet Joe McCollam

**Joe McCollam** — Founder & Principal Naval Architect
- **Photo:** 2026/01/Screenshot-2026-01-08-223303.png
- **Qualifications:** BEng (Hons) Yacht & Powercraft Design / Naval Architecture — Solent University, Southampton — 1993–1996
- **Authorised Surveyor:** YDSA (Yacht Designers & Surveyors Association) · Mecal · SCMS · MCA (Maritime & Coastguard Agency) · Irish Department of Marine
- **RNLI Service:** Lifeboat Coxswain — Red Bay All Weather Lifeboat · RNLI Crew Member since 1996 (Age 17)

Joe McCollam is the founder and principal Naval Architect and Marine Surveyor of McCollam Marine, bringing over 30 years of hands-on experience in naval architecture, boat building, and marine surveying. He holds a BEng (Hons) in Yacht and Powercraft Design/Naval Architecture from Solent University, Southampton (1993–1996), where he developed a comprehensive foundation in vessel design, hydrodynamics, structural engineering, and marine systems.

Joe is professionally qualified to carry out surveys and naval architecture services for major certifying authorities including the YDSA, Mecal, SCMS, and is authorised by the MCA and the Irish Department of Marine to carry out Stability Assessments. His work spans vessel design, performance predictions, stability analysis, pre-purchase surveys, insurance surveys, damage assessments, and specialist surveys on GRP, wood, aluminium, and steel vessels.

Beyond his professional practice, Joe serves as an RNLI Lifeboat Coxswain. Having first joined the Royal National Lifeboat Institution at age 17, he later returned to his home station at Red Bay where he now commands the All Weather Lifeboat. This ongoing operational experience — working with vessels in challenging real-world conditions — directly informs the practical, safety-focused approach he brings to every marine engineering project.
