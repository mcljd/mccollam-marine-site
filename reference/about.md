## Body content

McCollam Marine was established in 2011 to provide Naval Architecture and Marine
Surveying Services. It is a Naval Architecture and Marine Surveying business which
caters for a wide range of vessel types and operations on both the commercial and
leisure fleet throughout Ireland, UK, Europe and Worldwide. The company is based in
the beautiful Glens of Antrim on the North Coast of Ireland and has 20+ years
experience in Naval Architecture, Boat Building, Boat Surveying and Consultancy.

McCollam Marine is an authorised surveyor for the major Certifying Authorities
including the YDSA, YBDSA, Mecal, SEAFISH and the MCA.
