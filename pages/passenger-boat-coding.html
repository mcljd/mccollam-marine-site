Passenger Boat Coding

Expert guidance and survey support for passenger vessel coding and compliance with the relevant Codes of Practice across Northern Ireland, Ireland and the UK.

McCollam Marine are an Authorised Surveyor for carrying out passenger boat coding inspections and completion of the necessary paperwork for the YDSA, YBDSA and Mecal — the leading Certifying Authorities for Passenger Boat Coding.

01What is passenger boat coding?

Passenger vessel coding is the process by which small commercial vessels carrying passengers are assessed and certified for safe operation. In the UK and Ireland this is governed by the relevant Codes of Practice — including the MCA’s Code of Practice for Small Commercial Vessels and the equivalent Irish codes.

All UK-owned Small Commercial Vessels must comply with the relevant MCA Codes of Practice. If you wish to charter your boat or use it commercially you must comply — this includes occasional as well as permanent commercial use, and covers sport-fishing boats, RIBs used for public rides, and boats taking workers out to wind farms and oil rigs.
