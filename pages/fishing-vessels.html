Fishing Vessels

Specialist surveys, stability and compliance services for commercial fishing vessels across Northern Ireland, Ireland and the UK.

Commercial fishing vessels operate under some of the most demanding regulatory requirements in the maritime sector. McCollam Marine delivers surveys, stability assessments and compliance documentation that meet the requirements of the MCA, SFPA and SEAFISH.

01Our fishing-vessel services

SURVEY

Fishing Vessel Survey

Condition and statutory surveys for vessels of all sizes, from small inshore craft to larger offshore vessels.

STABILITY

Stability Assessment

Stability booklets and calculations to MCA and SFPA requirements, including inclining experiments where required.

TONNAGE

Tonnage Measurement

Tonnage measurement and certification for fishing-vessel registration and licensing.

COMPLIANCE

Compliance Support

Guidance on meeting regulatory requirements and managing deficiencies identified during surveys.

02Authorised surveyor

As an authorised surveyor for SEAFISH, McCollam Marine carries out statutory surveys and produces documentation required by the Sea Fisheries Protection Authority (SFPA) and the UK MCA. We are authorised by the MCA to carry out registration inspections on existing under-15m vessels coming onto the UK Register, and to carry out inspections on behalf of SEAFISH for new builds and existing vessels.
