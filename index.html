02 / THE PRACTICE

Three decades on the water,
and at the drawing board.

McCollam Marine was established in 2011 to provide naval architecture and marine surveying to the commercial and leisure fleet. Founded and led by Joe McCollam — BEng (Hons) Naval Architect, authorised surveyor and serving RNLI lifeboat coxswain — the practice pairs first-hand sea experience with rigorous, regulation-ready engineering.
